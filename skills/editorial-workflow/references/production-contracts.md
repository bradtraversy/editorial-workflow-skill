# Production Contracts

Use these contracts for outlining, drafting, reviewing, revising, packaging, and performance planning.

## Outline architect

### Inputs

- Approved brief
- Research dossier and source ledger
- Approved angle
- Author answers to material questions

### Output

Give every section:

- Heading concept
- Reader question
- Purpose
- Required claim IDs
- Firsthand material
- Example or demonstration
- Visual opportunity
- Link or call-to-action opportunity
- Transition logic

### Quality standard

- Solve the reader's problem in a logical order.
- Lead with substance rather than generic context.
- Preserve the approved angle.
- Include only sections that earn their place.
- Treat headings as promises.
- Use hierarchy for meaning, not keyword insertion.
- Do not copy competitor structure.

## Writer

### Inputs

- Approved brief, angle, and outline
- Research dossier and source ledger
- Voice packet
- Destination constraints

### Rules

- Draft one coherent article, not a stitched summary.
- Use verified facts and bounded observations.
- Preserve uncertainty when it matters.
- Use concrete examples before broad lessons.
- Do not introduce a new thesis silently.
- Do not imply unsupported causation.
- Preserve `[SOURCE NEEDED]` and `[AUTHOR INPUT NEEDED]` flags.
- Keep calls to action useful and proportional.
- Keep private source paths out of public copy.

### Mode adjustments

#### Search guide

Answer the task directly, explain important edge cases, use demonstrations, and avoid a long promotional introduction.

#### Project story

Center the problem, constraints, decisions, result, and real complications. Include technical detail only when it advances the story.

#### Opinion

State a real position, address the strongest counterargument, and distinguish preference, prediction, and evidence.

#### Personal essay

Build from firsthand material, protect privacy, preserve uncertainty, and do not force a framework or lesson.

#### Comparison

Apply declared criteria consistently, state what was tested, and recommend by context rather than naming a universal winner.

#### Timely analysis

Establish what changed, separate confirmed effects from expected effects, and record dates and current status.

#### Case study

Show the initial state, intervention, evidence, and limits. Do not claim more causation than the evidence supports.

## Review board

Reviewers report findings and do not rewrite the draft.

Use this format:

```md
## R-01: Short finding title

- Severity: critical | high | medium | low
- Location: section, line, or quoted phrase
- Problem:
- Evidence:
- Recommendation:
- Confidence: high | medium | low
```

Always include strengths to preserve and a verdict.

### Fact reviewer

Use `$editorial-fact-reviewer` to check material claims, dates, numbers, names, quotations, freshness, inference labels, and source scope.

### Technical reviewer

Use `$editorial-technical-reviewer` to check code, commands, terminology, required steps, security, privacy, and documented or observed behavior.

### Reader reviewer

Use `$editorial-reader-reviewer` to check whether the piece solves the reader problem, reaches substance, progresses logically, uses examples well, and remains skimmable without becoming shallow.

### Voice reviewer

Use `$editorial-voice-reviewer` to compare the draft with the voice packet and approved author-owned sources.

### Argument reviewer

Use `$editorial-argument-reviewer` to check thesis support, definitions, counterarguments, reasoning, limits, and conclusions.

### Search reviewer

Use `$editorial-seo-reviewer` only when discovery matters. Check intent, accurate titles and headings, related questions, useful links, metadata, and structured-data fit.

## Revision editor

### Inputs

- Brief
- Approved angle and outline
- Draft
- Review reports
- Source ledger and voice packet

### Process

1. Group duplicate findings.
2. Resolve critical and high findings first.
3. Compare conflicts with the brief and evidence.
4. Accept, reject, or defer each material finding.
5. Create a revision without overwriting the draft.
6. Preserve unresolved flags.

Write clean revised copy and a private decision log with accepted, rejected, and deferred findings, remaining questions, and claims still requiring verification.

## Packaging role

Create only assets required by the brief and destination:

- Title options
- Meta title and description
- Slug recommendation
- Social copy
- Hero or Open Graph brief
- Screenshots and diagrams
- Code samples
- Pull quotes
- Internal and external links
- Related content
- Call-to-action placement
- Canonical destination
- Syndication plan
- Refresh plan

Do not publish or transmit anything.

## Performance role

Use only after publication when real analytics are available. Review search impressions, queries, click-through rate, referral traffic, reader feedback, conversions, stale claims, and missing sections suggested by real demand.

Recommend leaving the piece unchanged, adjusting packaging, improving a section, adding an asset, updating evidence, consolidating, or retiring. Do not modify live content automatically.
