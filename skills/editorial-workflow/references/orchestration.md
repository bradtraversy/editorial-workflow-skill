# Orchestration

Coordinate separate agents or isolated role passes without leaking conclusions between them.

## General rules

- The managing editor owns state and integration.
- Parallelize independent research tracks and reviews.
- Keep outline, draft, revision, and approval sequential.
- Give each role the minimum relevant context.
- Assign one output artifact and clear acceptance criteria.
- Do not reveal the desired review conclusion.
- Do not let a review role modify the draft.
- Report results through artifacts and short status summaries.

## Multi-agent research

Possible independent tracks:

- Search and intent
- Primary sources
- Audience and community
- Firsthand experience
- Technical investigation
- Counterarguments

Each track receives the brief, one narrow question, mode and depth, approved source boundaries, output shape, and acceptance criteria.

The research synthesizer starts only after required tracks finish.

## Multi-agent reviews

Possible independent roles:

- Facts
- Technical accuracy
- Reader usefulness
- Voice
- Argument
- Search intent

Reviewers receive the brief, approved angle and outline, draft, source ledger when relevant, and their role contract. Do not provide the writer's self-assessment.

Every role reports its outcome, artifact, sources or tests, blockers, and follow-up needed.

## Single-agent fallback

When isolated agents are unavailable:

1. Finish and save the current role artifact.
2. Clear role-specific assumptions from the working summary.
3. Load only the next role contract and required artifacts.
4. Review from evidence rather than drafting memory.
5. Never describe the writer's own pass as independent.

For standard or cornerstone work, tell the user when true independence was unavailable.

## Failure handling

Pause when:

- Required evidence is unavailable.
- Author input is essential to truth or angle.
- Reviewers return materially conflicting findings.
- A critical factual, technical, privacy, or legal problem remains.
- The destination or audience changes enough to invalidate the brief.
- The next step requires external transmission or destructive action.

Record the blocker in `status.md` and state the smallest decision needed to resume.
